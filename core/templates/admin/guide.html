How the panel works

The administration homepage groups records by application. Select a section name to open its list. Search boxes, date navigation and filters appear where supported. Select a row to inspect or edit it. Add appears only where you have permission to create a record. Missing sections or buttons usually reflect your role; entering a direct URL does not grant access.

Save validates and stores the form, then returns to its list. Save and continue editing keeps the saved record open. Save and add another opens a new blank form. An error above or beside a field means you must correct the form before it can save. Bold field labels normally indicate required fields; blank optional fields are not an instruction to guess information.

For dropdowns, select an existing record that belongs to the same organization or parent company. A plus icon, when available, creates a related record; it does not bypass permissions. Tick boxes in a list only when you intend to run an available bulk action on those exact rows. Review the action description and any confirmation screen before submitting.

Some sections are intentionally read-only. Attendance corrections, leave decisions, payroll approvals/finalization, employee separation and document operations use the main HROS workflows so that authorization, explanations and audit history are preserved. Saving an admin form is not a substitute for those workflows.

Recommended setup order

  1. Organization: define ownership, default time zone and currency.
  2. Company references: create companies, and branches/divisions/teams/grades/cost centers only where your business uses them.
  3. HR references: create work schedules, departments, positions, employment types, work locations and approved holidays.
  4. Verified accounts and access: let users register/verify through the configured sign-in provider, then authorize their organization membership and role. Grant staff access only to approved admin operators.
  5. Employees: create employment records and link the correct verified account; set their department, manager, join date, location and schedule.
  6. Leave and pay: enter approved entitlements, recurring payroll components and period adjustments, then review attendance and balances before payroll.

Your role may expose only part of this setup. Ask an authorized platform or organization administrator to complete the remaining steps; do not create substitute records in another organization.

Accounts and authorized access

Active account
The account may sign in, subject to provider verification and other account controls.
Staff status
Permits entry to the administrative panel. The guide uses the same active-staff access requirement as the panel.
Groups and permissions
Determine which operations the account may perform. A role name alone is not sufficient; its configured permissions matter.
Active organization membership
Determines which tenant’s records a non-platform user may access. It is separate from staff status and role groups.
Employee link
Connects a verified login to an employment record for the employee portal. It is not an admin-access grant.
Superuser
Global platform access. Reserve this for explicitly authorized platform administrators.

Example: an approved HR operator needs an active verified account, an active membership for Example Services, the reviewed HR role permissions and Staff status for this panel. An ordinary employee does not need Staff status. Only platform administrators may grant privileged account access or maintain shared permission groups here.

With Neon Auth, registration, email verification and password recovery happen through HROS’s provider-backed screens. A local Django password does not reset a Neon password. Keep generated neon_… usernames unchanged; put human names in the name fields. Never paste passwords, verification codes or API keys into notes, reasons or chat.

The AI assistant separately requires runtime AI configuration and either active organization membership or administrator status. Seeing this guide does not change any user’s permissions.

Daily operations and a payroll example

  1. Review the daily attendance register. For a missed punch, use a correction request with the actual date/time and a specific explanation; an authorized reviewer decides it.
  2. Review pending leave through Leaves. Confirm entitlement and dates before approving/rejecting. The Leave decisions section is evidence of a decision, not the approval form.
  3. Before monthly payroll, resolve attendance exceptions, check approved leave, review component assignments and approve valid period adjustments.
  4. Preview/generate payroll through the main application. Have an independent authorized reviewer approve it, then finalize with the relevant permission. Use reversal when a finalized result needs correction.
  5. For offboarding, use the employee’s Deactivate action with separation date/reason. Retain attendance and pay history; deleting identities is not the offboarding workflow.

Calculation example: monthly basic pay 310000.00 in a 31-day month gives 10000.00 per calendar day. One absent day plus one half day equals 1.5 absent-equivalent days and a 15000.00 absence deduction. Basic pay after that deduction is 295000.00, before eligible overtime, earnings and other deductions. Approved leave does not itself create an absence deduction. This is an arithmetic example, not a statement of employment or tax policy.

Employee documents, attendance anomaly reviews, notifications and announcements are managed from their authorized main-application pages; they are not additional editable admin sections just because the underlying application supports them.

Troubleshooting

A dropdown is empty
Check that the reference record exists, is in the correct parent organization/company and is accessible to your role. Ask an authorized operator to create or assign it.
A related-record or duplicate error appears
Use references from the same organization. Check code/name uniqueness and existing employee/type/year entitlements. Do not create near-duplicate records to get around validation.
A field is grey or an Add/Delete button is absent
The field may be automatic, the record may be applied/finalized, or your role may not permit the operation. Use the documented workflow; do not attempt to edit history.
A user can sign in but cannot see company records
Check active organization membership, assigned permissions and, for self-service pages, the employee-account link. Staff status alone is not tenant access.
A saved change is not reflected in finalized payroll
Final payroll preserves its reviewed snapshot. Follow the authorized reversal/correction process; do not edit payroll history.
A name or sign-in problem remains
Confirm the correct verified email and account link. Name fields are for display, not identity matching. Use the HROS password reset/verification screens for Neon accounts.
