Capabilities

Engineered for Seamless HR Management

Everything needed to operate daily attendance and disburse monthly payroll with absolute precision.

One-Tap Daily Attendance

Register morning check-ins and evening check-outs with a single click. Seamlessly tag Present, Half-Day, Approved Paid Leave, or Unexcused Absence. Total working hours compute automatically.

• 1-Click Punch • Precise Timestamps • Status Classifications

Automated Loss of Pay (LOP)

Formula-driven daily rate calculation prorated by the exact days of the current calendar month. Half-days auto-deduct 0.5 day's wage while preserving approved paid leaves.

Printable PDF Salary Slips

Generate formal employee salary slips with corporate headers, itemized gross pay, deduction breakdown, and net salary. Formatted for instant browser print and PDF archiving.

Staff Directory & Roles

Full employee management: names, designations, basic salaries, contact details, join dates, and active/inactive toggles with instant search and status filtering.

Audit-Grade Monthly Reports

Review monthly attendance totals, leave records, and payroll summaries with access tailored to your role. Export reports for accounting and management reviews.

• Monthly Summaries • Print-Ready Layout • Total Expense Rollup

How It Works

The Three-Step Lifecycle

From the morning punch to the month-end salary slip, HROS automates the entire HR workflow.

01

Daily Punch Logging

Employees check in and out, or the HR administrator rapidly assigns daily status (Present, Half Day, Leave, Absent) with 1 click.

02

Continuous Aggregation

HROS continuously aggregates attendance logs, monitors hours worked, and tabulates absent days with zero manual arithmetic.

03

One-Click Payroll & PDF Slips

Review the monthly summary, generate the official payroll register, and print or export individual salary slips as PDF files.
